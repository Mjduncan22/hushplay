Privacy Policy

For the Project Virtue browser extension, app and website · Effective October 17, 2026

  • Virtue works on your device. It mutes the player and skips scenes, and never records, copies or uploads video, audio, captions or subtitles.
  • Looking up a video or title in the shared database is anonymous.
  • You don't need an account. If you make one, we store your email address, your free-trial hours and your subscription status.
  • Sharing what Virtue finds is off until you agree to it.
  • We don't sell data, show ads, use analytics or track you across websites. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card.

1. What Virtue does on your device

To find swearing, Virtue reads YouTube captions and the subtitle files streaming services load when you turn subtitles on. This happens only on your device; the text is used to work out when to mute and then discarded. Your settings are stored on your device (Chrome may sync them between your own devices).

2. What is sent, and when

Virtue never sends captions or subtitles themselves, audio or video, your browsing history, or your personal "Also mute" and "Never mute" words.

3. Who handles data for us

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in.
  • Stripe processes payments under its own privacy policy.
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.

4. Who can see what you share

Anyone using Virtue can see shared timestamps, but not who submitted them. Reviewers who check submissions can see the submitter's display name. Shared timestamps are marked not verified until a verified reviewer checks them.

5. How long data is kept

Your account, trial hours, subscription record and shared timestamps are kept until you ask us to delete them. Deleting your account deletes your timestamps and trial record. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires.

6. Your choices

  • Use Virtue without an account (your free hours are then counted on your device only).
  • Turn off "Use the shared database" to stop all lookups.
  • Turn sharing on or off at any time.
  • Cancel your subscription any time from your account page.
  • Ask us to delete your account and everything linked to it.

7. Children

Families use Virtue to make viewing more suitable for children, and filtering never needs an account. Accounts, sharing and subscriptions are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Use of data

Data is used only to run Virtue: filtering, the shared database, your free trial and your subscription. It isn't sold or transferred to anyone, isn't used for advertising, and isn't used to decide anyone's creditworthiness. Virtue's use of data follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

9. Contact and changes

Questions or deletion requests: michael.j.duncan22@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a change affects what is shared, Virtue will ask for your consent again.
