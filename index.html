For families who still love movies

Keep the story. Lose the language.

Project Virtue mutes swearing the moment it's said and skips the scenes you'd rather not see, on YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ and more.

Get Project Virtue See how it works

On YouTube, word by word

Virtue reads a video's captions before you get to each line, works out exactly when each swear is spoken, and mutes just that word. The rest of the sentence plays normally. Swear words are starred out in the captions too.

  • Choose what counts: strong swearing, mild swearing, taking God's name in vain, slurs, plus your own words.
  • Works with captions off. You don't have to watch with subtitles.

Movies and TV, scene by scene

On streaming services, Virtue mutes swearing found in the subtitles and skips scenes using timestamps shared by the community: nudity, sexual content, graphic violence, or whatever you choose.

  • Verified or not, you'll know. Timestamps a verified reviewer hasn't checked yet are clearly marked, and you can turn them off.
  • Tag as you watch. A key press marks a missed word or scene, and your tags help everyone once they're reviewed.

It only controls the player. Virtue mutes and skips the way your remote would. It never records, copies or uploads video, audio, captions or subtitles, and it doesn't change the services' videos.
