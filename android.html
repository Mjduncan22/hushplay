HushPlay for Android

Works on Android phones and on Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV.

This is a test version. It isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android will ask you to allow installing it.

Install

  • Phone: open the download link on your phone, download the file, open it and tap Install. If asked, allow your browser to install apps.
  • Google TV / Fire TV: install the free Downloader app on the TV, enter the download link, and install.
Download for Android

The download link is shared with testers directly while HushPlay is in testing.

Movies & TV apps

In HushPlay, tap V → Movies & TV apps and turn on the two settings it asks for. If Android says Restricted setting, open App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings and try again.
